Problem Deconstruction

The Real-World Reality of Problem F-02

Why traditional financial planning spreadsheets and generic AI chatbots fail.
❌ Why Spreadsheets Fail
• Static 8% Return Myth: Ignores sequence-of-returns risk and bear markets.

• Flat Inflation Assumption: Higher education (9%) and medical (11%) outpace general CPI (6%).

• Hidden Goal Trade-Offs: Cannot calculate the mathematical drop in Goal B when Goal A is funded.

• Stated vs Actual Spend: Ignores weekend splurges and payday lifestyle creep.
❌ Why LLMs & Chatbots Fail
• Financial Hallucinations: Generic LLMs invent savings totals and EMI math.

• No Stochastic Modeling: Cannot execute 2,000 Monte Carlo paths in a text prompt.

• Unverifiable Advice: Lacks mathematical audit trails or reproducibility guarantees.

• Zero Banking Interactivity: Disconnected from real statements and UPI payment rails.
💡 The F-02 Imperative: Users need an interactive tool to evaluate competing goals, modify assumptions, simulate shocks, and understand consequences over time.

Planning Under Uncertainty

2,000-Draw Stochastic Monte Carlo Engine

Simulating market volatility, correlated assets, and variable inflation.
📈 Correlated Asset Returns
• Cash & Liquid Funds: Low volatility, guaranteed floor.

• Fixed Income / Debt: Stochastic yield modeling.

• Domestic Equity: Geometric Brownian Motion with correlated market drift.

• Gold & Real Estate: Non-correlated diversification benefits.
🏷️ Category-Specific Inflation
• General CPI: Modeled at ~5.5% stochastic drift.

• Higher Education: Accelerated drift at 8.5% - 10.0%.

• Healthcare & Medical: Modeled at 9.0% - 11.5%.

• Lifestyle & Tech: Flat to moderate replacement inflation.
🎯 Common Random Numbers
• Fair Comparison: Compares Plan A vs Plan B on identical random draws.

• No Noise: Probability differences reflect pure decision quality, not simulation variance.

• Standard Error Display: Every metric shows Monte Carlo precision (e.g. ±0.9 pp).
📊 Confidence Bands: Output delivers P10 (Stress test floor), P50 (Expected median), and P90 (Optimistic ceiling) net worth trajectories.

Operating Strategy

Operating Engine: Value Mode vs Turbo Mode

Empowering users to toggle between defensive resilience and aggressive milestone acceleration.
🛡️ Value Mode (Prudent / Defensive)
• Primary Objective: Capital preservation, zero debt stress, and guaranteed liquidity.

• Emergency Buffer: Strict 6-month liquid reserve floor strictly defended.

• Savings Allocation: Stable +25% monthly savings rate.

• Discretionary Cap: Relaxed daily allowance.

• Best For: Volatile incomes, early-career savers, high-debt households.
🚀 Turbo Mode (Goal Accelerator)
• Primary Objective: Maximum goal compounding velocity and fast-tracked milestones.

• Emergency Buffer: 3-month buffer + aggressive SIP compounding.

• Savings Allocation: Boosted +40% monthly savings velocity.

• Discretionary Cap: Trims 20% from unneeded leaks redirected to goals.

• Result: Achieves BMW bike & home goals 6–9 months sooner!
